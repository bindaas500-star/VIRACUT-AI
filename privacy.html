Privacy Policy — ViraCut AI

Effective date: October 6, 2026

ViraCut AI ("the app") is a mobile video editor. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Account information (only if you sign in)

If you choose to sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive the basic profile you approve during sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo. This is used only to identify you inside the app (e.g. showing your name on the Profile tab).

Your sign-in session is stored only on your device. Signing out removes it from the device.

2. Your media stays on your device

Videos, photos, music and voice recordings you import or record are processed locally on your device. The app currently has no cloud backend, so your media is never uploaded to our servers — because there are none.

3. Projects, settings and usage

Projects, preferences, plan status and AI-generation counters are stored in your device's local storage. They never leave your phone unless you export or share a file yourself.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In — only if you tap "Continue with Google". Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Facebook Login — only if you tap "Continue with Facebook". Governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

The app currently contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics.

5. Permissions

The app may request access to your media library (to import videos/photos), microphone (for voiceover recording) and camera roll/files — only when you use those features, and only on your device.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because data lives on your device, uninstalling the app deletes everything. To remove your local sign-in session any time, use Sign out on the Profile tab. For any deletion request about data you believe we hold, contact us below.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will address it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app grows (for example when a cloud backend or payments are added). The current version will always be available at this page inside the app.
